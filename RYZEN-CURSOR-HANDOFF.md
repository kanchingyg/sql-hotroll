## Prompt 1: TypeScript CDN Range Gate (`src/cdn-range-gate.ts`) & Multi-PoP Simulator (`testbeds/cdn-sim.ts`)

```text

   - `mintStreamLease(claims, secret)` and `verifyStreamLease(token, secret, nowMs, expectedFileId)` using HMAC-SHA256 for `X-SQ-Stream-Lease` tokens that survive multi-minute downloads and R2/S3 multipart uploads after a 5-minute edge token rotates.
   - `compilePolyglotSqlSchema()` emitting SQLite WAL tables `files_v1` and `files_v2` with `_row_ver` and `_lc` JSON complement columns plus loop-free bidirectional `AFTER INSERT` triggers.

2. `testbeds/cdn-sim.ts` (~100 LOC):
```
